NASA-GIST Core Validation Site

South Korea’s first Core Validation Site (CVS), at Hampyeong: one 1 km grid cell measured from the ground, from drones and from satellites, to check the soil moisture maps of NASA SMAP and NASA-ISRO NISAR.

Site
1 km × 1 km cell, six kinds of land cover
Ground
47 stations, 82 soil moisture and temperature sensors, every 5 minutes since June 2025
Drones
1 m maps every one to two weeks in season: true colour, NDVI, thermal, LiDAR, L-band radiometer
Satellites
HLS, Landsat, MODIS, Sentinel-1, NISAR (beta)
  • In the portal
  • Ground, drone and satellite views side by side, date by date
  • Each station’s series and its soil properties
  • Data files on request

Global 30m-ET

Prithvi-OpenET: monthly evapotranspiration at 30 m from Landsat and Sentinel-2 imagery, estimated with a geospatial foundation model. The Korean Peninsula is open now as a preview; other regions are produced on request.

Resolution
30 m, monthly
Record
April 2013 – August 2026, 160 monthly maps
Coverage
Korean Peninsula now; any other land area on request
Status
Paper under review. Until it is accepted the maps are shown down to 120 m, and at 30 m in eight sample areas
  • In the portal
  • Mean annual ET, the seasonal cycle, or any single month
  • Click a place for its monthly series and full record
  • Request a region

Hybrid Dual-Channel Algorithm (HDCA)

L-band soil moisture that retrieves the vegetation scattering albedo instead of assuming it: HDCA solves soil moisture, VOD and a time-varying effective scattering albedo together from SMAP brightness temperatures, with first-order canopy scattering, so dense forests no longer read as persistently wet.

Grid
36 km EASE-Grid 2.0, global land
Record
Daily, April 2015 – December 2025
Variables
Soil moisture, vegetation optical depth (VOD), effective scattering albedo
Status
Paper accepted in Remote Sensing of Environment
  • In the portal
  • Daily, monthly and climatological maps on a globe
  • Click a cell for its full daily record of all three variables
  • Five validation sites marked

SMAP Time-Variant Error

How wrong is each SMAP soil-moisture value? The random error of every retrieval from triple collocation with ASCAT and GLDAS in a moving window, and, where triple collocation fails, predicted from SMAP’s own variables by LGBM and DNN models, with flags that say which is which and why.

Grid
36 km EASE-Grid 2.0, global land
Record
Every overpass, 6 a.m. and 6 p.m., February 2015 – November 2023
Variables
Soil moisture, TCA error σ, σ gap-filled by LGBM and by DNN, four TCA flags
Status
Published in GIScience & Remote Sensing (2025); 2022 on Zenodo, other years on request
  • In the portal
  • Overpass, monthly and climatological maps on a globe; where the error could be computed, filled, or not
  • Click a cell for its nine-year record with the four flags
  • Zenodo link, citation and collaboration contact
