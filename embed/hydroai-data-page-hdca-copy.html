Hybrid Dual-Channel Algorithm (HDCA)

L-band soil moisture that retrieves the vegetation scattering albedo instead of assuming it: HDCA solves soil moisture, VOD and a time-varying effective scattering albedo together from SMAP brightness temperatures, with first-order canopy scattering, so dense forests no longer read as persistently wet. Global, daily, 36 km, 2015–2025.
